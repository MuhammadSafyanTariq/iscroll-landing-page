1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how iScroll (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use the iScroll mobile application and related services (the “Service”). By using iScroll, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect account details (name, username, email), profile content, media you upload, social interactions (likes, comments, follows), messaging data, device/app diagnostics, and analytics events needed to operate and improve the Service.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide core features (accounts, feed, messaging, notifications), security and fraud prevention, customer support, analytics, and legal compliance.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with service providers that help us run the app (for example hosting, authentication, crash reporting, and analytics) under appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention & Security

We retain data while your account is active and as needed for legal and security purposes. We use industry-standard protections; no method of transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can delete your account in the app settings. For privacy requests, contact us using the in-app support options.

7. Children

iScroll is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
